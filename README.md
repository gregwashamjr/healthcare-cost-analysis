## 🧠 Business Problem
Healthcare costs vary widely depending on payer and encounter type. This project analyzes reimbursement gaps to identify opportunities to reduce patient burden and improve coverage efficiency.

## 🎯 Key Insights
- Costs are concentrated in ambulatory and urgent care encounters  
- Coverage varies significantly across payers  
- Self-pay encounters contribute heavily to uncovered costs  
- Reimbursement differs across encounter types
